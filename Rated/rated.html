🐂Bulls & 🐄Cows

Instructions

1. Choose a class based on your preferred difficulty level.

2. Guess the 4-digit secret number with no repeated digits or leading 0.

3. Bull: correct digit in the correct position; Cow: correct digit in the wrong position.

4. Complete the game within the attempt and time limit of your selected class.

5. Earn points based on your selected class and try to achieve the highest score. 🏆
